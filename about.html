About the Firm

Experience You Can Build On

Vivas & Vivas Law Office pairs decades of financial and regulatory depth with a battle-tested litigation practice — one firm, two generations of counsel, working for the people and businesses who rely on us.

Our Firm

A full-service practice, personally handled.

Vivas & Vivas Law Office is a full-service Manila-based law firm anchored in civil and criminal litigation — with a particular focus on family law — and a dedicated banking and regulatory practice. We combine a founding partner's four decades at the intersection of law, finance, and regulation with a junior partner's versatile, courtroom-tested litigation experience.

Every matter is handled personally by the partners. We give honest advice, prepare thoroughly, and advocate vigorously — because our clients come to us not just for competence, but for results.
