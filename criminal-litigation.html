Overview

Defense and prosecution, at every stage.

We represent both the accused and private complainants in criminal proceedings — from inquest and preliminary investigation before the prosecutor's office, through bail, arraignment, and trial, to appeal. For the accused, that means asserting every right and defense the law provides. For complainants, it means building a complaint that prosecutors will find probable cause on, and actively assisting the prosecution as private prosecutor through trial.

Criminal cases are won by preparation and procedure: a timely counter-affidavit, a well-argued petition for review, a bail application filed at the right moment, a demurrer when the prosecution fails to carry its burden. We know these levers because we work them constantly — this is one of the two pillars of our litigation practice.

In financial and economic crimes — estafa, bouncing checks, and fraud-related prosecutions — our forensic accounting capability lets us trace transactions and test the numbers behind the charge, whether we are prosecuting or defending.

Scope of Work

What We Handle

Inquest & Preliminary Investigation

Immediate assistance for persons arrested or under investigation — counter-affidavits, motions, and petitions for review before the Department of Justice.

Bail & Provisional Remedies

Applications for bail and reduction of bail, motions to quash warrants, and remedies against unlawful arrest and detention.

Trial Defense

Full trial representation for the accused — cross-examination, objections, demurrers to evidence, and a defense built on the prosecution's burden of proof.

Private Prosecution

For complainants: drafting complaint-affidavits, pursuing probable cause, and prosecuting the civil aspect of the criminal case through trial.

Financial & Economic Crimes

Estafa, violations of BP 22 (bouncing checks), qualified theft, falsification, and fraud cases — backed by CPA-lawyer forensic analysis.
