Overview

Vigorous advocacy in civil disputes.

Civil litigation is the backbone of our practice. We represent plaintiffs and defendants in disputes over money, contracts, and property before the first-level courts, the Regional Trial Courts, and the appellate courts — and before that, in the demand and negotiation stage where many disputes can still be resolved on favorable terms.

Every case begins with an honest assessment: what your claim or defense is actually worth, what the evidence supports, and what it will take to win. From there we prepare deliberately — well-documented pleadings, carefully built evidence, and witnesses who are ready — because civil cases are won in the preparation long before they are won in the courtroom.

Where a dispute has a financial dimension — questioned accounts, disputed valuations, or hidden assets — our forensic accounting practice, led by a CPA-lawyer, gives our litigation work an analytical edge few firms can match.
