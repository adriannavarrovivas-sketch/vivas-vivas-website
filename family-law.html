Overview

Discreet counsel for life's hardest decisions.

Family law is the particular focus of our civil litigation practice. We guide clients through declaration of nullity and annulment of marriage, legal separation, custody and support disputes, and the recognition of foreign divorce — proceedings that are as emotionally demanding as they are legally technical.

These cases require more than sympathy. Petitions for nullity under Article 36 of the Family Code, for instance, rise or fall on how well the evidence of psychological incapacity is developed and presented. We prepare each petition deliberately — working with you, and where needed with expert witnesses, to build a case the court can grant.

Because family disputes often carry property consequences — the liquidation of the absolute community or conjugal partnership, support computations, and disputed assets — our firm's financial depth, including a CPA-lawyer-led forensic practice, is a real advantage when the numbers are contested.
