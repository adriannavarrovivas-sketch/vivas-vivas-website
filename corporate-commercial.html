Overview

Counsel that keeps your business moving.

We maintain legal retainers for companies across a wide range of industries — manpower agencies, engineering firms, airlines, BPO companies, security agencies, food manufacturers and packagers, car parts manufacturers, importers, contractors, and transport companies. For these clients we are the first call for anything legal: contracts, compliance, employment questions, disputes, and everything in between.

Our corporate work is practical by design. We help businesses incorporate and stay compliant, register where regulators require it — including AMLC registration and PCAB licensing for contractors — and paper their transactions so problems are prevented rather than litigated. When disputes do come, the same firm that wrote the contract fights for it in court.

Transactions with significant financial stakes benefit from our firm's distinctive depth: legal and financial due diligence conducted under one roof by a CPA-lawyer, with forensic accounting support when the numbers need a closer look.
