About the Firm

Trusted advocacy, grounded in integrity.

Vivas & Vivas Law Office is a full-service Manila-based law firm built on a simple commitment: to give every client honest advice, careful preparation, and vigorous representation. We handle each matter personally, keeping you informed at every stage.

Our practice is anchored in civil and criminal litigation — with a particular focus on family law — and a dedicated banking and regulatory practice drawing on decades of experience at the intersection of law, finance, and regulation. Whatever your matter, our partners bring focused attention and courtroom experience to your case.

  • Personal attention You work directly with the partners handling your case.
  • Clear communication Plain-language advice and transparent fees.
  • Diligent preparation Thorough, well-documented work product.
